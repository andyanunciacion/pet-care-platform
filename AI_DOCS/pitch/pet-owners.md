## How to run an owner conversation (≈ 10–15 min)

1. **Listen first (7–10 min).** Use the questions in §3. Get *stories* about the last time they needed a vet.
2. **Show (3 min).** A few slides (§4), or better, the live staging site on your phone. Let them search for something they actually need.
3. **Ask (1 min).** Ask to keep in touch for launch (§8), and whether they know other pet owners or groups you could talk to.
4. **Write notes right after** using the template in §10.

**Where to find owners:**
- Local pet Facebook groups (message the admins first)
- Pet shops and grooming salons (ask the owner's permission)
- Clinic waiting rooms (only with the clinic's permission)
- Parks and pet events around Clark and Angeles
- Friends of friends

Tips:
- Ask about **what actually happened** ("the last time your pet got sick…"), not "would you use an app that…?"
- Owners of **exotic pets** (rabbits, birds, reptiles) and anyone who had a **night-time emergency** usually feel the pain most. Seek them out.

## 3. Interview questions (ask BEFORE pitching)

**About them and their pets**
- What pets do you have? How many? How long have you had them?
- Which vet do you usually go to? How did you first find them?

**The last time they needed a vet**
- Tell me about the last time you needed a vet. What happened? How did you find the clinic?
- Have you ever gone to a clinic and found it closed, or that it didn't offer what you needed? What did you do?
- Have you had an emergency at night or on a holiday? How did you find help?
- Was there a service you had trouble finding (kapon, surgery, X-ray, dental, a vet for exotic pets)?

**How they decide**
- What info did you wish you had *before* going? (prices, hours, which animals they treat, which vet is on duty…)
- How do you decide if a clinic is good? Who do you trust for recommendations?
- Which apps or groups do you use for pet stuff? (FB groups, Google, TikTok, Messenger…)

**Records and reminders**
- How do you keep track of vaccinations and deworming? (the clinic's card or booklet, phone photos, memory?)
- Have you ever missed a vaccine or deworming date?

**After showing the demo** (reaction questions)
- Try searching for something you actually needed recently. Did you find it?
- What's confusing or missing?
- Would you trust the hours shown? What would make you trust them more?
- Would you use this on the website, or would you only use it as an app?
- Would you make an account to save your pets' info and get reminders? What would stop you?

## 6. How it works — owner journeys (use these as demo scripts)

**Journey 1 — "My dog is sick and it's 9 PM on a Sunday"**
1. Open PAW → tap **Emergency / 24-hour** and **Open now**.
2. See the nearest open clinics on the map, sorted by distance.
3. Tap a clinic → check that it says *"Open now · hours confirmed 5 days ago"*.
4. Tap **Call**, then **Directions** → Waze opens.

**Journey 2 — "I want to have my cat spayed (kapon)"**
1. Search **"kapon"** → results show clinics offering spay/neuter for cats.
2. Compare **price ranges** and locations.
3. Tap **Messenger** to ask about the schedule.

**Journey 3 — "Who treats rabbits around here?"**
1. Filter by animal: **Rabbit**.
2. Only clinics that treat rabbits show up. Check which services cover rabbits.
3. Save time: no more calling clinics one by one.

**Journey 4 — "The hours on this page are wrong"**
1. Tap **Report incorrect info** → pick "Hours" → type what you saw.
2. PAW or the clinic checks it and fixes the page.

## 7. Questions owners may ask (FAQ)

| They ask… | You answer… |
|---|---|
| "Is it free?" | Yes, and you don't need to sign up to search. |
| "How is this different from Google Maps?" | It's built for pets: search by service, animal, open now, emergency. It shows price ranges and when the hours were last confirmed. Directions still open in Google Maps or Waze. |
| "How do I know the info is correct?" | Every page shows when its hours were last confirmed, and verified clinics manage their own pages. If something's wrong, report it in one tap. |
| "Which clinics are on it?" | We're starting with vet clinics in Angeles, Mabalacat, and Clark, aiming to list every clinic in the area before expanding. |
| "Do clinics pay to be at the top?" | Normal results are ranked by relevance and distance. Any paid placement will always be labeled "Sponsored". |
| "What about groomers, pet shops, boarding?" | Vets come first. Other pet services are planned later. |
| "What do you do with my data?" | You don't need an account to search. When accounts come, your data will be handled under the Data Privacy Act and never sold. |
| "When is the app coming?" | The website works on your phone from day one. The app will come later with reminders and bookings. |
