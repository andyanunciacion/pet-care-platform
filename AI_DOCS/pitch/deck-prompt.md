# Prompt for building the pitch decks in Claude (web)

Upload `clinics.md` and `pet-owners.md` with this prompt, and fill in the [brackets]. Re-upload the files whenever they change.

```
I'm the founder of [PAW — working name], a pet-care discovery platform launching in
Angeles City, Mabalacat and Clark (Pampanga, Philippines). We're pre-launch and I'm
interviewing vet clinics and pet owners. Attached are two pitch kits.

Create two PowerPoint decks (16:9):
1. Clinic deck: follow the slide outline in §4 of clinics.md.
2. Pet owner deck: follow the slide outline in §4 of pet-owners.md.

Content rules:
- Use §1, 2, 5, 6 and 9 as source material. Put the 30-second pitch and the FAQ /
  objection answers (§7) in the speaker notes, not on slides.
- Do NOT put the interview questions (§3), "the ask" (§8) or notes templates (§10) on slides.
- Add a short "Who we are" slide before the closing slide in the clinic deck:
  [your story / local connection].
- Honesty: no invented statistics, users, testimonials, partner clinics or dates.
  Label the sample monthly report "Sample". Show "Coming later" features without dates.

Design:
- Shown in person on a [laptop / tablet / phone]: big text, few words per slide.
- Style: friendly, warm, trustworthy, local. Colors: [your colors or "suggest a palette"].
  Logo: [attached / use the text "PAW" for now].
- For phone mockups, draw simple placeholder screens (search bar, filter chips, clinic card
  with an "Open now" badge and "Hours confirmed 5 days ago") labeled "Preview — design not
  final". I'll replace them with real screenshots later.
- Placeholders to keep visible: [Brand name], [Contact details], [QR code].
```
